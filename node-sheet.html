Reading the sheet

Four things the drawing makes visible

Seven model calls per email

extract, the classify fallback, score, summarize, categorize, propose_event and outline each cost one round trip. That is the number that sets the bill for a full run, and the reason every one of those stages is independently skippable from the CLI.

The gates come before the spend

Both expensive branches sit behind a deterministic gate. is_scheduling_related decides whether the calendar nodes run at all, and the outline node is gated on read status and ¬no_reply. Cheap checks guard costly work, never the other way round.

Retrieval fuses three channels

Keyword over fts5, cosine over stored vectors, and graph neighbours — merged by reciprocal rank rather than a tuned weighting, so adding a channel later does not mean re-tuning the other two. Each hit records which channels found it.

Side effects are few and late

Only three nodes change anything outside the database, and all three are Google calls. Nothing sends email. A proposed event stays SUGGESTED until a person approves it, and once approved or declined a re-run will not touch it again.
