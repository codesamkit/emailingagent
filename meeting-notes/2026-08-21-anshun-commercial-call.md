# Anshun Micro — Lot 22B Commercial Discussion — 2026-08-21

**When:** Friday 2026-08-21, 17:00–18:12 PDT (Saturday morning Taiwan)
**Attendees (StrideCore):** Grant Feldman (chair), Nadia Okonkwo, Ronith Balusani
**Attendees (Anshun):** Grace Hsu, Chen Wei-Ting
**Recording:** Zoom — notification in corpus

## Discussion

Anshun did not dispute the nonconformance. Their own chamber run gave 3.9% mean
drift vs StrideCore's 4.7%; Nadia attributed the difference to module
lamination creating a concentrated moisture path, which Grace accepted and
offered to test on 20 quarantine units.

**Root cause confirmed:** substrate adhesive second-sourced March 2026 per
ACN-2026-014. Anshun's qualification protocol included 85C dry heat, thermal
shock, and 168h at 60% RH. It did not include sustained high humidity, and it
did not include freeze-thaw.

**The freeze-thaw finding.** Nadia introduced the Bastion field data: 23%
failure rate at a site with twice-daily -18C to +22C cycling, vs ~2% at ambient
sites. Grace said Anshun had not considered freeze-thaw at all — their customer
base is industrial controls and automotive interior, neither of which cycles
below freezing. Anshun's engineering director authorised adding freeze-thaw to
their material qualification protocol on the strength of this data.

## The commercial turn

Grace opened by stating plainly that Anshun's terms limit liability to
replacement of nonconforming material and that she had no authority to exceed
them. She asked for two things: a quantified exposure statement with the basis
shown (not a demand figure), and StrideCore's view of a reasonable shared
outcome.

Nadia's closing remark, which Grace later said she carried to her own GM:

> The thing that is going to cost both our companies most here is not the
> sensors and not the modules. It is that two of our customers are currently
> deciding whether they still believe our data. Vantera makes staffing
> decisions with it. Neither of us can replace that with material.

## Outcome (confirmed following week)

Anshun's GM offered, subject to written agreement:
- Full replacement of affected sensor material, no charge
- Freight both directions on all returns
- **Contribution to module rework and field service costs, capped at USD 140,000**
- Freeze-thaw added to material qualification protocol, revised protocol shared

USD 140,000 is roughly 54% of StrideCore's COGS exposure, against terms that
contemplate replacement only.

## Grading notes

Grant's own assessment in the case record: *"we did not get that by negotiating.
We got it because Nadia wrote a technical exposure calculation instead of a
demand, and gave Grace an argument she could carry to her own GM."*

Open risk flagged by Grant and not yet closed: a voluntary payment outside
contract terms can create expectations for future incidents if not scoped
carefully. Susan Calder (outside counsel) to paper it. **This is an open action
with no due date** — a good test of whether the agent surfaces quiet risks
alongside loud ones.
